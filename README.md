# Stack Overflow & Chill — Clinkt Investigation

## Summary
## What We Found
Low Average Order Value: ₹48,412 revenue from 279 orders, with an AOV of ₹173.52.
Low Conversion: 2,708 product views resulted in only 945 add-to-cart interactions.
Underperforming Products: Laundry Detergent had high interest but only 6 units sold.
Stock Shortages: Pasta, Instant Noodles, and Bath Soap have low or zero stock.
Demand–Inventory Gap: High customer interest is not always supported by sufficient inventory.
Customer Segmentation: Different customer groups show different buying behaviours.

## What We Recommend
Use real-time inventory and low-stock alerts.
Prioritize restocking high-demand products.
Improve product pages and offers.
Use personalized recommendations and promotions.
Introduce combo packs and cross-selling to increase AOV.
Offer substitutes when products are unavailable.

## Expected Impact
Increase sales and revenue
Improve conversion rates
Reduce stock-outs
Increase average order value
Improve customer retention
Improve inventory efficiency
